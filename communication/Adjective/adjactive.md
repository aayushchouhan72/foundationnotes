## What is an Adjective?

An **adjective** is a word that **describes or modifies a noun or pronoun**. It tells us **what kind, which one, how many, or whose**.

**Examples:**

- She has a **beautiful** dress.
- I bought a **red** car.
- He is an **honest** man.

---

# Types of Adjectives

## 1. Descriptive (Quality) Adjective

Describes the quality or nature of a noun.

**Examples:**

- She is a **kind** girl.
- It is a **large** building.
- He is an **intelligent** student.

---

## 2. Quantitative Adjective

Tells **how much** (used with uncountable nouns).

**Examples:**

- I have **some** water.
- There is **little** milk left.
- We need **enough** sugar.

---

## 3. Numeral Adjective

Tells **how many** or **in what order**.

### (a) Cardinal

Shows number.

- Two books
- Five students
- Ten apples

### (b) Ordinal

Shows position.

- First prize
- Second floor
- Third chapter

### (c) Indefinite Numeral

Does not give an exact number.

- Many people
- Several students
- Few books

---

## 4. Demonstrative Adjective

Points out a specific noun.

**Examples:**

- **This** book is mine.
- **That** house is beautiful.
- **These** flowers smell good.
- **Those** cars are expensive.

---

## 5. Possessive Adjective

Shows ownership.

**Examples:**

- **My** phone
- **Your** bag
- **His** bike
- **Her** dress
- **Our** school
- **Their** house
- **Its** tail

---

## 6. Interrogative Adjective

Used with nouns to ask questions.

**Examples:**

- **Which** book do you want?
- **What** color do you like?
- **Whose** pen is this?

---

## 7. Distributive Adjective

Refers to individual members of a group.

**Examples:**

- **Each** student received a certificate.
- **Every** child was happy.
- **Either** road leads to the station.
- **Neither** answer is correct.

---

## 8. Proper Adjective

Formed from proper nouns.

**Examples:**

- **Indian** culture
- **American** food
- **French** language
- **Chinese** tea

---

## 9. Compound Adjective

Two or more words joined together to describe a noun.

**Examples:**

- A **well-known** actor
- A **ten-year-old** boy
- A **full-time** job
- A **high-speed** train
