Important SQL Concepts Used
Concept	Purpose
WHERE	Filters individual rows
GROUP BY	Groups similar records
COUNT()	Counts records
SUM()	Calculates total
AVG()	Calculates average
MAX()	Finds maximum
MIN()	Finds minimum
HAVING	Filters grouped results
ORDER BY	Sorts results
LIMIT	Restricts number of rows
BETWEEN	Checks a range
IN	Matches multiple values
AND / OR	Combines conditions

Important formulas
Use these formulas consistently throughout the assignment:
Calculation	Formula
Total sales	SUM(quantity * price)
Total quantity	SUM(quantity)
Average price	AVG(price)
Average order value	AVG(quantity * price)
Total discount	SUM(discount)
Average discount	AVG(discount)
Transaction count	COUNT(*)

One important clarification: Your table has a discount column, but the INSERT records appear to store discount as a total amount per transaction, not a per-unit discount. Therefore, the total sales formula above is quantity * price. If you want net sales after discount, use SUM(quantity * price - discount) instead.
